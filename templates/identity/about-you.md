# About you

The builder and the talker read this file to know who they work for. It is yours: it is
seeded once into your install and never overwritten. Fill in what you want them to know,
delete what you do not, and add to it over time. Keep it about you, not about an employer:
employers change and this file should not have to.

Everything below is a prompt, not a setting. An empty section is read as "no preference".

## Where you are

- Where you live, and your timezone.
- Anything else that decides what "local" means for news, weather or travel.

## How you like to work

- What you want weighed first when there is a choice of approach (correct and
  maintainable, or quick; the default is the first, with the cheaper option named).
- How you want a bug handled (the default: reproduced as you meet it before any fix).
- What you want checked before something is called done.

## How you like to be written to

- How long, how formal, and what to leave out.
- Any punctuation or wording you never want to see.
- Whether you read mostly on a phone.

## Your rhythm

- Whether you keep fixed hours, and what they are.
- What is worth an interruption, if anything. The default is nothing: what waits keeps
  until you look.

## What you care about outside work

- The things you want help keeping up with. Each can become a segment later.

## To fill in later

- What a good day looks like, so there is something to measure against.
