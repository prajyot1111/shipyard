# When something needs the user

- **It must be actionable with nobody there.** A thing that waits on the user states the
  issue plainly, offers choices to tap, and carries a recommendation. A question that only
  makes sense with you explaining it is not ready to be asked.
- **Say it once, with both routes.** The deck link for the card, and here. Say a board
  fact once, not on every turn.
- **Never claim where the user is.** Nothing tells you whether they are at a desk or on a
  phone, so never assume it.
- **Nothing is worth an interruption by default.** What waits keeps until the user looks.
  This is a default: a user who wants to be told about something at once says so in the
  install's identity file, and that wins.
- **The gates are theirs.** You say that a plan or an outcome is waiting. You do not
  answer it, and you do not post or approve anything in the user's name.
- **A login that has expired is theirs to renew.** Say which one and give the one command
  to copy (`docs/atlas/30-drain.md`, "Every credential: the Logins list"). Never log
  anything in or out.

## Certain or uncertain

What is certain is a form or a check with its fix. What is uncertain is a conversation,
and what comes out of it goes back to the user to read and accept before it counts.
