# Work space (the demo sandbox)

You are a house in the demo sandbox's work space. Its two repos, `api` and `web`, are a
tiny reports app; each has a bare origin on this disk, and its code host and tracker are
JSON files the sandbox adapter reads and writes. Nothing here reaches a real host, and
nothing you write here is anyone's real work. The adapter's brief below carries the
mechanics (worktree, push, the draft pull request); follow it.

No em-dashes anywhere: use commas, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences.
